## Why this needs training

See `V101_V102_BASELINE.md` for the three measured failures. Independent face and crown classifiers can both be confident on a nose, so their execution order decides its layer. The blue crown's front/back presence predictions disagree and disable crown routing. Nose/beard and inward crown-cap geometry were missing from the old authored training data.

## Changes

- Add one 14-class categorical head: face, inner/outer hair, inner/outer beard, eyewear, headphones, hat body/band layers, brim and royal crown. Headwear and ownership probabilities are marginals of that same distribution, recomputed after affine interpolation. A pixel cannot simultaneously have high face and crown probability.
- Joint attention reads the adjacent front/back head views of a single player. Players remain separate. This candidate supports the established ordered `front_left`, `back_left` pair.
- Author 0–3-cell crown caps connected to side teeth, open top centres, varied palettes and texture. Author shaded noses, eyebrows, moustaches/beards, inner and outer hair/beard geometry, symmetric and asymmetric examples. Real user images are not gradient inputs.
- Mix 60% new joint samples with 20% legacy ownership and 20% legacy headwear/textured-hair negatives. Retain glasses, headphones and hat/brim examples.
- Feed confident inner-hair evidence into the top-layer decision only when both projected views support it. Protect explicitly identified genuine outer hair from the crown-only top solver. No filename rules, colour identities or unconditional symmetry operations.
- Freeze all 386 v101 tensors and train only the added branch. Keep the foreground model, body parser and old heads unchanged.

## Training and reproducibility

`bash dense_uv_parser/run_v102_training.sh` launches 4,800 steps, batch size 6 players / 12 views, AdamW learning rate 0.0002 with cosine decay. The first checkpoint is at 200, then every 600 steps. Output directory must not already exist; the launcher does not overwrite runs or silently resume an incompatible run.

`runs/v102_multiview_20260906/` contains status, source snapshots/hashes, source-split provenance, baseline validation, per-checkpoint validation, optimizer state and model checkpoints. Every checkpoint verifies that all frozen tensors are identical to v101.

Each checkpoint runs the three new real development cases plus the existing 14 real regressions, saving final UVs and separate inner/outer front/back renders. These are review probes, not training labels. Semantic pixel metrics are distinct from final UV quality. The beard metric is a semantic inner-layer vote, not an end-to-end geometry score.

Validation source identities are disjoint from training but have been reused in earlier development. New procedural seeds do not make this an untouched real-world test set.

## Release policy and verification

The default `v101_release.json`, its checkpoint/pipeline, and all dataset `*_result_v101.png` stay pinned. Training does not promote a checkpoint or replace user results. Candidate release requires checking crown cap recall/openings, nose layer, beard continuity, back-view hair corners, old glasses forehead, headphone colour contamination, hat band/brim continuity and body preservation.

Before launch: full 2-step optimizer/checkpoint smoke, strict checkpoint reload and 17-case inference smoke; existing regression suites plus the new paired-context, categorical exclusivity, affine-boundary, data-label and renderer geometry tests. Detailed receipts are in `runs/v102_audit_20260906/`.


## Final inference review corrections

The 4,800-step run finished in 544.7 seconds. Its checkpoint keeps all 386 v101 tensors unchanged. Source code and checkpoint provenance remain separate: the training source snapshot is commit `5013096`; later inference corrections do not change learned weights.

Actual rendered outer-semantic evidence now resolves remaining ambiguous non-headwear hair-top cells. An uncertain front footprint can be resolved by the clear back view. True outer hair is tested for preservation; confident/uncertain hats, crowns and confirmed headphones retain their specialized geometry paths.

The frozen, previously validated headphone presence detector selects the original ownership/colour path for confirmed headphone groups. This preserves the old phone RGB exclusion and hidden-hair repair; the new joint head is used for the other groups. The selection is semantic and filename independent. It also survives affine projection.

The first training-time real probes reused base output archives that predated the headphone gate. Final real validation now runs the complete current model instead. Treat `real_accepted_4800` as the final 17-case review; earlier real probe folders are development history, not release evidence. Pixel validation/gradients/checkpoints are unaffected by this review correction.


Facial-hair routing additionally pools the predicted inner/outer beard identity across each player's two views. Confident component evidence assigns one layer, while strong conflicting identities preserve real holes and neighbouring objects. A component assigned inner also vetoes residual outer texels after splatting; pixels outside the base projection cannot recreate a duplicate beard fringe. No colour or symmetry template is used. The reported beard now has no duplicated lower-front outer texels and its inner material is sampled from observations without imposing a mirrored texture.
